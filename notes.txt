* Jacobian sparsity

  There are 3 issues that make the Jacobian non-sparse:

  * Weak rate contributions via Ye
  * Screening plasma state contributions via Z^2 and Ye
  * evolving e instead of T, and the coupling at constant e

  Literature:

  * WinNet says that they ignore the screening composition dependence
    https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2012MNRAS.424.2222P/abstract

    "Furthermore, derivatives of the screening correction factor
     (Section 2.5) are considered to be zero" (p. 10)

  * Pakmor et al. 2012 (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2012MNRAS.424.2222P/abstract)
    evolve Σ Zᵢ² Yᵢ explicitly, decoupling it from the species derives
    to make things sparse again

  Can we do something similar to Pakmor et al. for e and T?

  * We can derive an [A(T)]^{n+1/2} after the advective update
    from e^{n+1}
